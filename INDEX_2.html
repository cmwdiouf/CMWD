GAINDE-AI

Automated Management of Environmental Information and Data using AI
Real-time hydrocarbon leak detection and monitoring platform for offshore facilities

The problem

An undetected offshore leak can cost an operator and a country hundreds of billions of XOF within months: production shutdown, repair, suspension of maritime activity, sea cleanup and compensation of coastal populations. Detection is usually late and reactive.

Our approach

IoT sensors installed on wellheads, manifolds, pumps and safety valves stream concentration, pressure, temperature and turbidity data. An AI layer scores each reading and raises an alert before a small anomaly becomes a disaster: from late reaction to immediate, proactive anticipation.

Prototype version: the sensor readings shown in this demo are simulated.

Architecture

  • Sensor data integration (IoT)
  • AI implementation (anomaly scoring)
  • Platform interface design
  • Operational modes (alerting, audit, reporting)
  • Execution and continuous improvement

Current status and limits

  • This demo uses simulated sensor streams, not real field data.
  • The AI score shown here is a threshold-deviation model used to demonstrate the interface; it is not a trained production model.
  • No real leak detection claim is made at this stage.

Roadmap

  • Continuous data creation to train and validate the model on real and open Earth observation data
  • Satellite layer to cross-check sensor alerts with remote sensing of the sea surface (planned, not yet implemented)
  • Evolution towards an onshore platform
  • International deployment and inter-industry collaboration
